- [ ] [EffortlessBuildingMixinConfig.java + EffortlessBuildingBatchMixinConfig.java] Neither mixin fires for NORMAL-mode vanilla placement.
  - why: In NORMAL mode (no Line/Wall/Cube/etc.), the client input handler doesn't intercept and vanilla `BlockItem.place()` is the only path.
